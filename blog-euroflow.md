# 7 Ways to Speed Up Your Monthly Expense Reporting

Managing expenses shouldn’t be a full-time job. Whether you’re a freelancer or part of a global team, efficiency is key to getting reimbursed faster. Follow these seven steps to streamline your workflow using the **EuroFlow App**.

### 1. Snap Photos Immediately
Don’t wait until the end of the month. Use the **EuroFlow App** to snap a photo of your receipt the moment you get it. This ensures you never lose a VAT-compliant receipt and keeps your digital records organized.

### 2. Monitor Your Status
Understanding the difference between **Pending** and **Approved** helps you predict when money will hit your account. Our latest guide explains the "Flagged" status so you can fix errors before they cause delays.

### 3. Categorize on the Go
Assign categories like **Travel**, **Meals**, or **Office Supplies** immediately after uploading. Grouping expenses in real-time prevents a massive backlog during audit season.

### 4. Prioritize Digital Invoices
Whenever possible, upload original PDF invoices rather than photos of screens. Digital files are easier for the system to read, reducing manual data entry errors.

### 5. Set a Weekly Review Ritual
Dedicate 10 minutes every Friday to review your "Draft" expenses. Handling small batches of data is more accurate than rushing to meet a month-end deadline.

### 6. Verify Local Currency
Always check that the currency matches your receipt before clicking submit. Correcting currency mismatches early prevents reconciliation issues for the finance team.

### 7. Perform a Final Audit
Before the final submission, double-check that the total amount in the app matches the physical receipt. A 30-second final review can be the difference between an instant payout and a rejected claim.
